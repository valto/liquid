# Start with your perspective

Liquid Software asks how information, human decisions and meaningful capabilities can remain useful while interfaces and implementations change. Choose a guide that starts with the questions you bring to that idea.

Each guide explains the implications for its reader, a concrete situation, the limits of the proposal and a short reading path. You can read it yourself, print it, or give its tailored prompt to your agent. The guides share the same concepts and source papers; a different perspective changes the explanation, not the underlying definitions.

## How this resource fits together

The [supplied umbrella paper](https://valto.github.io/liquid/whitepapers/00-beyond-the-constraints-of-software/) sets out the overall direction. The [concepts](https://valto.github.io/liquid/concepts/) can be understood and used independently. The [Economics of Execution v3](https://valto.github.io/liquid/whitepapers/economics-of-execution/v3/) develops the implementation decision, and the [sixteen companion research drafts](https://valto.github.io/liquid/whitepapers/) explore distinct aspects in depth.

The conceptual source is Valto Loikkanen. The writing and research are AI-assisted. These guides interpret a proposed framework; illustrative calculations and research drafts do not establish that the full architecture has been deployed or independently validated.

You can move between perspectives as your question changes. For a broad introduction first, choose [general understanding](https://valto.github.io/liquid/readers/general/). For a reusable handoff, open [the agent prompt](https://valto.github.io/liquid/agents/).
