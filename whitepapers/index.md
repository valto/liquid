## Expanded research edition

## Continuous intelligence and stable experience

[Continuous Intelligence, Adaptive Evolution, and Stable Experience](https://valto.github.io/liquid/whitepapers/continuous-intelligence/) is the supplied 18-page companion, version 1.0, dated 28 November 2026. Its complete PDF, page-separated transcription, original Markdown, three SVG diagrams and source ZIP are retained together. It adds the temporal dimension: how systems review, learn, introduce alternatives and preserve familiar experiences.
