# An Intelligence-Native Reference Architecture

## Different concerns, different rates of change

| Concern | What persists | What may adapt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Principal | Identity, relationships and authority | Representatives and providers |
| Information | Originals, accepted revisions and provenance | Indexes, interpretations and projections |
| Capability | Purpose, outcomes and commitments | Intelligent, deterministic and hybrid realizations |
| Interaction | Agreed meaning and disclosure boundaries | Languages, formats and contextual presentation |
| Governance | Human decisions and external obligations | Operational choices within delegated freedom |

An individual and an entity are useful information-principal anchors. The requester may still be a human, agent or application. The immediate actor, represented principal, beneficiary and purpose remain distinct.

## A bounded request

1. Identify the need, requester and represented principal.
2. Resolve meaning and actual authority using shared context and guidance.
3. Select an existing, intelligent or hybrid fulfillment path.
4. Validate the accepted outcome, evidence, freshness and uncertainty.
5. Return the suitable representation and preserve commitments.
6. Observe demand and propose improvements within the same boundaries.

## Complementary frameworks

[PIOS](https://www.peecos.org/pios/master) and [EIOS](https://github.com/entity-core/eios) provide personal and entity information-framework proposals. [PAIX](https://github.com/privateaiexchange/paix-spec) explores interaction across participants. [CRD](https://valto.github.io/crd/) documents capabilities, while [MLE](https://valto.github.io/crd/mle.html) helps preserve meaningful scope.

These are related bodies of work with their own authority and scope. Adoption of one Liquid Software concept does not require adopting every framework.

## Learning, evolution and experience

The [Continuous Intelligence companion](https://valto.github.io/liquid/whitepapers/continuous-intelligence/) connects signals, persistent understanding, purposeful reviews, change decisions, evolution paths and outcome verification. Feedback can revise understanding and review policies without automatically changing operations.

Review purpose and depth are independent of frequency. A scheduled review and a material event trigger can complement each other. The meta-loop examines the quality of those reviews. Changes can improve a backend beneath a stable experience, introduce a parallel alternative, or deliberately retire a realization.

The source proposes nine logical objects: a signal, review policy, review record, change proposal, CRD, implementation version, experience composition, experiment or rollout, and outcome evidence. They describe relationships to preserve rather than a mandatory database schema. Facts, inferences, proposals, approved decisions and deployed implementations retain distinct statuses.

Its decision questions are **why, what, who, how, when, verification and learning**, preceded by identifying what needs checking. They help connect purpose, authority and execution. Candidate construction, validation, release, adoption and retirement remain separate effects; a trigger does not grant them all.

[Pulse CXM](https://www.pulsecxm.com/) supplies a customer-side application context connecting signals to coordinated work and verification. EIOS covers broader organizational continuity; PIOS covers personal owner-controlled context. A personally composed interface can use an organizational capability through authorized interactions without automatically transferring personal Core information.

CRD preserves capability meaning; Skills guide use; agents orchestrate; software or declared structures implement suitable parts. The proposed Reasoning Compilation Skill has its own requirements and governance. Describing this arrangement does not establish its implementation or authorize a deployment.

## Examine the integrated model

This is a reference architecture for testing. It is not a deployed system or a new mandatory protocol. [Research draft 15](https://valto.github.io/liquid/whitepapers/drafts/15-the-architecture-of-an-intelligence-native-world/) develops the boundaries and research questions. [Draft 14](https://valto.github.io/liquid/whitepapers/drafts/14-from-software-centric-to-information-centric-computing/) addresses transition from existing systems.
