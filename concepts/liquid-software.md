# Liquid Software

**Definition.** Implementations emerge, adapt, stabilize and retire around actual needs, within durable semantics and human-defined boundaries.

Software fluidity concerns the implementation of a capability. Meaning, accepted commitments, information ownership and authority must remain understandable as that implementation changes.

UI on the fly and backend on the fly describe creation or adaptation in response to context. Liquid Software extends the principle to integrations, retrieval and execution. A deterministic implementation can remain the best choice for years; fluidity includes choosing stability.

## A reversible lifecycle

A novel need may be fulfilled intelligently. Recurring demand can justify a validated query, cache, service or other optimization. Changed needs may require adaptation. Declining demand may justify retiring that realization while preserving the promised capability or deliberately revising its commitment.

## Examine the tradeoff

Measure quality, latency, cost, reliability, reversibility and the effort needed to preserve meaning. Changing software is useful when it improves fulfillment, not merely because generation is available.
