# The Self-Optimizing Information System

**Definition.** Observe actual fulfillment and propose useful changes to implementations and retrieval structures under explicit authority.

The system observes semantic requests, cost, latency, correctness, sensitivity and dependencies. An optimizer can identify candidates for specialization, adaptation, consolidation or retirement.

A scheduled optimization process is an example of operational organization, not a required nightly ritual. Frequency is one signal alongside commitments and total operating costs.

## Preserve acceptance and recovery

An optimization must retain the authority, meaning and evidence requirements of the original capability. Test representative queries, source freshness and rollback. Retiring a realization does not automatically authorize withdrawing a promised capability.

## Measure net benefit

Include the expense of generating, reviewing, operating and retiring an optimization. Compare it with the intelligent or existing path and retain a suitable fallback where the commitment requires one.

The objective is cumulative net value over a relevant horizon, subject to accepted quality, risk and authority. Include time to value, enabling intelligence, adoption and restoration, maintenance, flexibility and foregone opportunities. A lower runtime bill can conceal delayed or lost value. Use least-cost selection only when outcomes and timing are comparable.

Compare plausible demand and failure scenarios with existing tools, direct agents and hybrids. Optimization can apply to a capability's meaningful contract or to its internal operations; smaller components do not automatically become separate capabilities. Reassessment may justify keeping the current realization, selectively materializing another one or retiring an unneeded derived structure.


## Learning and change have different rates

[Layered Intelligence Cycles](https://valto.github.io/liquid/concepts/layered-intelligence-cycles/) distinguishes review purpose and depth from cadence. [Meta-Learning](https://valto.github.io/liquid/concepts/meta-learning/) evaluates missed signals, false triggers, review costs and reversals, including whether the optimizer changes too much or too little.

A trigger calls for examination within its scope. It does not automatically authorize production change. Candidate development, release, adoption and retirement can be separate decisions. The optimizer can recommend retaining a stable realization when transition effects outweigh improvement.

Measure internal throughput and external fitness together. [Outcome Verification](https://valto.github.io/liquid/concepts/outcome-verification/) returns to the beneficiary's original need; a completed task or lower inference bill is insufficient evidence that the problem improved.
