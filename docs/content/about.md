**Conceptual source and project owner: Valto Loikkanen.**

This is an English-first body of conceptual research. AI-assisted synthesis, research and drafting develop the source discussion into independently useful definitions and papers. Loikkanen, Valto (conceptual source). (2026). *Beyond the Constraints of Software: The transition toward an information-centric, intelligence-native world*. Conceptual White Paper 00, version 1.0, 26 November 2026. AI-assisted synthesis.

The paper's edition date is distinct from the website's actual publication date. Original PDF versions are retained rather than silently replaced. Research drafts are separately identified and have their own source text and bibliography.

## Status and scope

The supplied paper reports no independent literature review or empirical validation. The companion research drafts cite relevant prior work and propose tests; they have not undergone independent peer review. Architecture descriptions are proposals, not claims of live API, MCP or backend implementation.
