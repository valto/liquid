The transition toward an information-centric, intelligence-native world.

Conceptual source: Valto Loikkanen. Conceptual White Paper 00, version 1.0, dated 26 November 2026. The supplied PDF is retained unchanged, including its AI-assisted source statement.

The shared thesis

Information provides continuity. Human decisions define the boundaries. Intelligence fulfills needs, with software emerging as an optimization.

The paper connects four reversals: preserve appropriate source richness before storage anticipates every future question; retrieve around emerging needs; derive interfaces from context; and choose software implementations around useful capabilities.

Its twelve pages connect Liquid Software, adaptive interfaces, unknown future information value, adaptive retrieval, self-optimization, individual and entity principals, decision freedom, internet economics and practical transition questions.
