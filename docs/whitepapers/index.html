The supplied umbrella paper

Beyond the Constraints of Software is the original supplied twelve-page Conceptual White Paper 00, version 1.0. Read its PDF or complete text transcription.

The expanded economics paper

Liquid Software: The Economics of Execution, supplied version 2.0, dated 27 November 2026, adds a nine-page framework for time to value, lifecycle investment, reversible realizations, modularity and demand-driven information processing. Read the original PDF, complete transcription and related Capability Economics explanation. The existing umbrella and draft PDF versions remain unchanged.

Expanded research edition

The 33-page version 3.0, also dated 27 November 2026, expands the economics treatment with 31 chapters and 19 figures. It adds explicit scenario accounting, worked value and retrieval examples, capability portfolios and decision-maker maturity. Read its preserved original and complete transcription alongside v2. Valto approved publication of this edition and its related update under CC BY 4.0 on 27 November 2026.

Continuous intelligence and stable experience

Continuous Intelligence, Adaptive Evolution, and Stable Experience is the supplied 18-page companion, version 1.0, dated 28 November 2026. Its complete PDF, page-separated transcription, original Markdown, three SVG diagrams and source ZIP are retained together. It adds the temporal dimension: how systems review, learn, introduce alternatives and preserve familiar experiences.
