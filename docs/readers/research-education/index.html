Liquid Software for research and education

Separate conceptual proposals, source lineage and illustrative examples, and frame comparisons that can test the claims.

Conceptual source: Valto Loikkanen · AI-assisted synthesis and research

Read source text ↗Give this to your agent

Liquid Software is a connected conceptual proposal about information continuity, human authority, adaptive interaction and implementation choice. For researchers, educators and students, the main question is which claims can be tested, and how should the concepts be distinguished from their examples and related work?

The conceptual source is Valto Loikkanen. The supplied papers, AI-assisted companion drafts and these reader guides belong to the same body of work; they are not independent corroboration of one another.

Distinguish the units of the argument

The paradigm concerns adaptable realizations around durable meaning and decisions. Adaptive interfaces concern recipient and context. Source preservation concerns future evidential usefulness. Adaptive retrieval concerns evolving access and processing. Capability Economics concerns feasible strategies and cumulative net value.

These concepts can be evaluated independently. An experiment showing a useful retrieval cache would not establish the full architecture. A generated interface that users understand would not prove permission enforcement or economic superiority.

Follow the source and version lineage

Begin with the supplied umbrella paper, then a selected concept and its companion research draft. Economics of Execution v3 is a 33-page expansion retained alongside v2. Its broader formulation of maturity bias concerns limitations in the alternatives a decision-maker perceives. Its understanding, experience and implementation dimensions are conceptual, not a validated assessment scale.

The public research index identifies related original works and selected editions. Consult those originals for their own claims and methods. Other uses of the phrase “Liquid Software,” local-first work and malleable software are related material; their results should not be silently attributed to this framework.

Design a comparison that could change the conclusion

For one defined capability, establish acceptance criteria, the source evidence and the feasible baseline. Compare existing software, agentic and hybrid paths where relevant. Include elapsed time to accepted value, outcome quality, total cost, supervision, failure, latency and recovery.

For source preservation, introduce a later question and examine what retained evidence can actually support. For interfaces, test comprehension and accessibility across representations. For governance, test permitted and denied operations, conflicts and intervention. A study can support one dimension while exposing failure in another.

Treat economic examples as assumptions to vary

The v3 figures are worked illustrations. Vary demand, initial assurance, utilization, changing costs and transition conditions. A cost threshold over comparable outcomes is different from a forecast of adoption or a measured advantage. Repetition does not by itself establish that specialization will repay its remaining investment.

The paper's external examples also use different measures: feature use, project outcomes and qualitative transition reports. Keep their populations and limitations separate. They do not establish a pooled failure rate or a counterfactual agentic success rate.

Use the resource as a discussion framework

A seminar can ask different readers to examine the same capability through business value, technical contracts, information continuity and authority. Their summaries should differ in emphasis while preserving definitions and evidence status. This is also a useful test of the framework's claim that shared meaning can support different representations.

Report unknowns and negative results. Separate observed findings, interpretations, proposed mechanisms and illustrative scenarios. Practical evidence can broaden the alternatives a decision-maker recognizes without making any implementation category a measure of maturity or quality.

A short reading path

  1. Beyond the Constraints of Software. Read the supplied argument before evaluating its parts.
  2. Research and Related Work. Trace related original works and their separate claims.
  3. The Architecture of an Intelligence-Native World — Research Draft 15. Examine the integrated proposal and its research questions.
  4. Liquid Software: The Economics of Execution — Version 3.0. Read pages 26 and 30–33 for maturity distinctions, empirical questions, lineage and references.

Questions to explore

  • Which specific claim is being tested, and what would count against it?
  • Are the compared paths feasible and judged by the same accepted outcome?
  • Which cited sources are independent, and what did each actually measure?
  • Which parts of an explanation are definitions, examples, hypotheses or observations?

Resource foundations:
- Attributable information, accepted meaning and human decisions provide continuity while interfaces and implementations adapt.
- A capability is a meaningful ability with an accepted outcome. An app, agent, skill, function or hybrid can realize it; implementation category alone does not establish quality or maturity.
- Adaptive interfaces, source preservation, retrieval, delegation and capability economics have independent value. Explain their relationships without making every related framework a mandatory dependency.
- Begin with languages, formats and shared meaning. A schema may be generated, inferred or negotiated where useful. Accurately distinguish a proposed interaction from the guarantees of a specific claimed protocol implementation.
- Compare feasible strategies by cumulative net value, timing, full lifecycle costs and retained flexibility. Some quality, safety, authority and reliability requirements must be satisfied before first use.
- Source preservation and processing investment are distinct. Derived structures retain their provenance and do not acquire source authority through convenience or location.

Attribution and evidence:
- Valto Loikkanen is the conceptual source. The writing and research are AI-assisted.
- Distinguish the supplied umbrella paper and Economics of Execution v3 from the sixteen labeled companion research drafts and these reader guides.
- Worked numbers and scenarios are illustrative. This body of work does not establish deployment or independent validation of the whole framework. Related original sources have their own claims, methods and limits.
- Report which pages and source sections you actually read. State any access limitation rather than implying you read unavailable material. Keep source facts, interpretations, proposals, examples and unknowns distinguishable.
