This is an English-first body of conceptual research. AI-assisted synthesis, research and drafting develop the source discussion into independently useful definitions and papers. The attribution does not claim exclusive invention of all component ideas or priority for the established term Liquid Software.

Suggested citation for the supplied umbrella paper

Loikkanen, Valto (conceptual source). (2026). Beyond the Constraints of Software: The transition toward an information-centric, intelligence-native world. Conceptual White Paper 00, version 1.0, 26 November 2026. AI-assisted synthesis.

The paper's edition date is distinct from the website's actual publication date. Original PDF versions are retained rather than silently replaced. Research drafts are separately identified and have their own source text and bibliography.

Status and scope

The supplied paper reports no independent literature review or empirical validation. The companion research drafts cite relevant prior work and propose tests; they have not undergone independent peer review. Architecture descriptions are proposals, not claims of live API, MCP or backend implementation.

Contribution and reuse

Contributions can clarify definitions, improve examples, verify sources and report meaningful contradictions.
