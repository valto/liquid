An Intelligence-Native Reference Architecture

Compose enduring information and authority with adaptive interaction, fulfillment and governed optimization.

Conceptual source: Valto Loikkanen · AI-assisted synthesis and research

Different concerns, different rates of change

Concern What persists What may adapt
Principal Identity, relationships and authority Representatives and providers
Information Originals, accepted revisions and provenance Indexes, interpretations and projections
Capability Purpose, outcomes and commitments Intelligent, deterministic and hybrid realizations
Interaction Agreed meaning and disclosure boundaries Languages, formats and contextual presentation
Governance Human decisions and external obligations Operational choices within delegated freedom

An individual and an entity are useful information-principal anchors. The requester may still be a human, agent or application. The immediate actor, represented principal, beneficiary and purpose remain distinct.

A bounded request

  1. Identify the need, requester and represented principal.
  2. Resolve meaning and actual authority using shared context and guidance.
  3. Select an existing, intelligent or hybrid fulfillment path.
  4. Validate the accepted outcome, evidence, freshness and uncertainty.
  5. Return the suitable representation and preserve commitments.
  6. Observe demand and propose improvements within the same boundaries.

Complementary frameworks

PIOS and EIOS provide personal and entity information-framework proposals. PAIX explores interaction across participants. CRD documents capabilities, while MLE helps preserve meaningful scope.

These are related bodies of work with their own authority and scope. Adoption of one Liquid Software concept does not require adopting every framework.

Examine the integrated model

This is a reference architecture for testing. It is not a deployed system or a new mandatory protocol. Research draft 15 develops the boundaries and research questions. Draft 14 addresses transition from existing systems.
