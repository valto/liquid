Core concepts

Eight independently useful concepts connect information continuity, adaptive interaction, evolving execution and human decision freedom.

Conceptual source: Valto Loikkanen · AI-assisted synthesis and research

Read source text ↗

These concepts describe different parts of the transition. Liquid Software concerns changing implementations; adaptive interfaces concern interaction; information preservation concerns what survives; and decision freedom concerns the authority within which choices can change.

Each definition is a research draft with its own scope, version, relationships and associated papers. The concepts are proposals for examination and testing, rather than normative requirements for every system.

The economics review candidate adds a ninth concept, Capability Economics, and proposes revisions to five related concepts. Their current candidate versions have not been published; the original public versions remain attributable to v0.1.0.

Independent concept

Liquid Software

Implementations emerge, adapt, stabilize and retire around actual needs, within durable semantics and human-defined boundaries.

↗Independent concept

The Adaptive Interface

Presentation and interaction respond to what is needed, by whom, where and why; how is a contextual result.

↗Independent concept

Capture for Unknown Future Value

Preserve appropriate source richness before anticipated questions determine the full extent of what remains.

↗Independent concept

Adaptive Information Retrieval

Interpret emerging questions and evolve retrieval structures around observed needs while preserving source evidence and agreed meaning.

↗Independent concept

Intelligence First, Software Second

Establish the ability to understand and fulfill a need before choosing where dedicated software provides a useful optimization.

↗Independent concept

Degrees of Decision Freedom

Higher-level decisions define the contextual space in which humans and delegated actors can make further choices.

↗Independent concept

The Self-Optimizing Information System

Observe actual fulfillment and propose useful changes to implementations and retrieval structures under explicit authority.

↗Independent concept

Documentation as Executable Foundations

Shared terminology, capability requirements and guidance preserve meaning while implementations and operational choices evolve.

↗Independent concept

Capability Economics

Compare feasible realizations by risk-adjusted cumulative net value over time, including full lifecycle costs and retained flexibility.
