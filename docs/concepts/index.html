Core concepts

Fifteen independently useful concepts connect information continuity, adaptive experiences, capability economics, purposeful learning, parallel evolution and outcome verification.

Conceptual source: Valto Loikkanen · AI-assisted synthesis and research

Read source text ↗

These concepts describe different parts of the transition. Liquid Software concerns changing implementations; adaptive interfaces concern interaction; information preservation concerns what survives; and decision freedom concerns the authority within which choices can change.

Each definition is a research draft with its own scope, version, relationships and associated papers. The concepts are proposals for examination and testing, rather than normative requirements for every system.

Release v0.2.0 adds a ninth concept, Capability Economics, and revises five related concepts using the expanded economics source. Their earlier public versions remain attributable to v0.1.0.

Release v0.3.0 incorporates the 33-page Economics of Execution v3 source, expands Capability Economics and Adaptive Information Retrieval, clarifies the source-attributed maturity terms and adds an optional capability decision record. Earlier paper editions and concept versions remain retained in the publication history.

The current review candidate adds six concepts developed from the supplied Continuous Intelligence companion: Layered Intelligence Cycles, Meta-Learning, Stability-Aware Evolution, Parallel Realizations, Reasoning Compilation and Outcome Verification. They complement the earlier nine concepts. Learning, release and adoption can happen at different rates; useful adaptation can preserve a familiar experience.

Independent concept

Liquid Software

Implementations emerge, adapt, stabilize and retire around actual needs, within durable semantics and human-defined boundaries.

The Adaptive Interface

Presentation and interaction respond to what is needed, by whom, where and why; how is a contextual result.

Capture for Unknown Future Value

Preserve appropriate source richness before anticipated questions determine the full extent of what remains.

↗Independent concept

Adaptive Information Retrieval

Interpret emerging questions and evolve retrieval structures around observed needs while preserving source evidence and agreed meaning.

↗Independent concept

Intelligence First, Software Second

Establish the ability to understand and fulfill a need before choosing where dedicated software provides a useful optimization.

↗Independent concept

Degrees of Decision Freedom

Higher-level decisions define the contextual space in which humans and delegated actors can make further choices.

↗Independent concept

The Self-Optimizing Information System

Observe actual fulfillment and propose useful changes to implementations and retrieval structures under explicit authority.

↗Independent concept

Documentation as Executable Foundations

Shared terminology, capability requirements and guidance preserve meaning while implementations and operational choices evolve.

↗Independent concept

Capability Economics

Compare feasible realizations by risk-adjusted cumulative net value over time, including full lifecycle costs and retained flexibility.

↗Independent concept

Layered Intelligence Cycles

Reviews need distinct purposes and depths as well as suitable cadence and triggers.

↗Independent concept

Meta-Learning: Review the Learning Process

Evaluate how the system notices, reviews and learns, including false triggers, missed signals and unnecessary change.

↗Independent concept

Stability-Aware Evolution

Compare improvements with transition effects, giving stable operation and familiar experience explicit value.

↗Independent concept

Parallel Realizations

Release, adoption and retirement can be separate decisions for compatible realizations of a capability.

↗Independent concept

Reasoning Compilation

Materialize formally checkable portions with validation, assumptions, provenance and invalidation conditions.

↗Independent concept

Outcome Verification

Connect signals, decisions and execution to whether the beneficiary’s original need improved.
