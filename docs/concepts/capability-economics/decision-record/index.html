Capability Decision Record

An optional editable record for capability outcomes, alternatives, evidence, costs, authority, review triggers and recovery.

Conceptual source: Valto Loikkanen · AI-assisted template from Economics of Execution v3, page 27

Read source text ↗

Optional working template derived from Valto Loikkanen's Economics of Execution, version 3.0, page 27. AI-assisted editorial adaptation. This is a conceptual documentation aid, not a CRD schema, implemented optimizer or grant of authority.

Field Record
Capability and version Meaningful identity, version and links to applicable requirements.
Accountable owner Principal and person or role responsible for the decision.
Required outcome Beneficiary, purpose and measurable acceptance criteria.
Feasibility boundary Quality, safety, reliability, latency, privacy, authority and other commitments.
Baseline Existing fulfillment, its actual results and remaining costs.
Alternatives Feasible manual, existing-tool, agentic, deterministic and staged/hybrid paths; doing nothing if legitimate.
Demand and horizon Observed demand, forecast scenarios, useful life, dates and demand uncertainty.
Benefits Attributable realized and forecast contribution; explain conversion of released capacity into value.
Costs Incremental enabling, build, operation, assurance, maintenance, transitions and retirement; distinguish sunk expenditure and shared-capacity allocation.
Risk and flexibility Downside constraints, failure scenarios and forgone alternatives; avoid counting the same loss twice.
Evidence Sources, dates, observations, assumptions, measurement limits and unresolved questions.
Selected strategy Initial realization, optional later stages, rationale and conditions for each transition.
Authority Who may approve, deploy, access information, commit expenditure or withdraw a service.
Revisit triggers Meaningful demand, quality, price, policy or source changes; estimated cost of reassessment.
Recovery and continuity Fallback, restoration, retained records, source evidence and commitments.
Decision and date Actual approval, dissent or open decision; no inferred authorization.

Use consistent units, periods and accounting boundaries. Unknown probabilities may be better represented by ranges and scenarios. Retaining an existing implementation is an eligible decision. An attractive expected value does not override a hard safety or authority constraint.
